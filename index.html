FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

KCW Pay is a cross-chain USDT payment platform that lets you send and receive USDT across multiple blockchain networks — Ethereum, Tron, BSC, Polygon, and more — with the simplicity of sending a message.

When you send USDT through KCW Pay, you send from your chain of choice. The recipient receives USDT on their preferred chain. Our smart contracts handle the conversion and delivery transparently, so both parties see exactly what's happening.

KCW Pay currently supports Ethereum, Tron, BNB Chain (BSC), Polygon, Arbitrum, and Optimism. We are actively working on adding more chains to expand cross-chain coverage.

Yes. KCW Pay is fully self-custody. You maintain control of your private keys and assets at all times. We never hold or access your funds — we only facilitate the cross-chain transfer process.

KCW Pay charges a small transparent fee for each cross-chain transfer. The exact fee is displayed before you confirm any transaction — no hidden charges. Network gas fees from the source chain also apply.

Most transfers settle within seconds to a few minutes, depending on the source and destination chain confirmation times. Tron and BSC transfers are typically the fastest.
